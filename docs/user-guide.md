## Getting Started

### Welcome to Pet Support App!

Pet Support App is your comprehensive companion for pet care, designed specifically for first-time pet owners. Our app provides real-time guidance, AI-powered assistance, and personalized care plans to help you navigate the challenges of pet ownership.

### Key Features

- **🤖 AI Chat Assistant**: Get instant answers to your pet care questions
- **📊 Health Tracking**: Monitor your pet's weight, medications, and milestones
- **🚨 Emergency Triage**: Quick assessment when your pet needs urgent care
- **📋 Personalized Care Plans**: Custom recommendations based on your pet's needs
- **🗺️ Local Resources**: Find vets, pet stores, and services in your area
- **🔔 Smart Notifications**: Reminders for medications, appointments, and care tasks

## Account Setup

### Creating Your Account

1. **Download the App**: Available on iOS App Store and Google Play Store
2. **Launch the App**: Tap on the Pet Support App icon
3. **Sign Up**: 
   - Enter your email address
   - Create a secure password (minimum 8 characters)
   - Fill in your first and last name
   - Accept the terms and conditions
4. **Email Verification**: Check your email for a verification link
5. **Welcome**: You're now ready to start using the app!

### Login Options

- **Email & Password**: Traditional login method
- **Biometric Authentication**: Use fingerprint or face recognition (available on supported devices)
- **Social Login**: Login with Google or Apple (coming soon)

### Profile Setup

Complete your profile to get personalized recommendations:

1. **Location**: Enable location services for local resource recommendations
2. **Pet Preferences**: Select the types of pets you're interested in
3. **Notification Preferences**: Choose which alerts you'd like to receive
4. **Privacy Settings**: Control data sharing and privacy options

## Adding Your First Pet

### Pet Profile Creation

1. **Tap "Add Pet"**: On the home screen, tap the "+" button
2. **Basic Information**:
   - Pet's name
   - Type (Dog, Cat, Bird, Fish, etc.)
   - Breed (if known)
   - Age or birth date
   - Gender
   - Current weight
3. **Photo**: Upload a profile picture of your pet
4. **Medical Information**:
   - Microchip ID (if applicable)
   - Allergies
   - Current medications
   - Vaccination status
5. **Save**: Your pet is now added to your profile!

### Managing Multiple Pets

- Add unlimited pets to your account
- Switch between pet profiles easily
- View all pets on the main dashboard
- Track individual health records for each pet

## AI Assistant Features

### Chat Interface

Our AI assistant is here to help 24/7 with your pet care questions:

1. **Access AI Chat**: Tap the chat icon in the navigation bar
2. **Ask Questions**: Type your questions about:
   - Nutrition and feeding
   - Behavior and training
   - Health concerns
   - General pet care advice
3. **Get Instant Responses**: Receive helpful, personalized answers
4. **Follow-up**: Ask additional questions for clarification

### Image Analysis

Upload photos for visual assessment:

1. **Tap the Camera Icon**: In the chat interface
2. **Select or Take Photo**: Choose from gallery or take a new photo
3. **Describe the Issue**: Add context about what you're concerned about
4. **Get Analysis**: AI will analyze the image and provide guidance

**Note**: AI analysis is for informational purposes only and should not replace veterinary care for serious concerns.

### Common Questions

The AI assistant can help with:
- "How much should I feed my puppy?"
- "Why is my cat scratching so much?"
- "Is this plant safe for my dog?"
- "What are the signs of separation anxiety?"
- "How do I introduce a new pet to my home?"

## Emergency Triage

### Quick Assessment

When you're concerned about your pet's health:

1. **Emergency Button**: Tap the red emergency icon
2. **Symptom Selection**: Check all symptoms your pet is experiencing
3. **Answer Questions**: Provide additional context about the situation
4. **Get Urgency Level**: Receive immediate guidance on urgency
   - **Critical**: Seek emergency care immediately
   - **High**: Contact vet within hours
   - **Medium**: Schedule vet visit soon
   - **Low**: Monitor and contact vet if worsens

### Emergency Contacts

Quick access to emergency resources:

- **24/7 Emergency Vets**: Find nearby emergency clinics
- **Poison Control**: Direct contact information
- **First Aid Guides**: Step-by-step emergency instructions
- **Pet Ambulance**: Available services in your area

### When to Use Emergency Triage

Use the triage system for:
- Sudden behavioral changes
- Injury or trauma
- Breathing difficulties
- Severe vomiting or diarrhea
- Suspected poisoning
- Any concerning symptoms

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Login Problems**
- Ensure correct email and password
- Check internet connection
- Reset password if needed
- Clear app cache and restart

**Sync Issues**
- Check internet connection
- Pull to refresh data
- Ensure app is updated to latest version
- Restart the app

**Notifications Not Working**
- Check notification permissions in device settings
- Ensure app is updated
- Verify notification preferences in app settings
- Restart your device

**Location Services**
- Enable location permissions in device settings
- Check if location services are turned on
- Ensure app has permission to access location
- Try restarting location services

## Tips for New Pet Owners

### First Week Essentials

1. **Establish Routine**: Consistent feeding and walking times
2. **Create Safe Space**: Designate a comfortable area for your pet
3. **Find a Vet**: Establish relationship with a local veterinarian
4. **Pet-Proof Home**: Remove hazards and secure dangerous items
5. **Start Training**: Begin basic commands and house training

### Building Confidence

1. **Ask Questions**: Use the AI assistant for any concerns
2. **Join Community**: Connect with other pet owners
3. **Track Progress**: Use health tracking to see improvements
4. **Celebrate Successes**: Document milestones and achievements
5. **Stay Informed**: Keep up with pet care best practices

### Long-Term Success

1. **Regular Check-ups**: Schedule annual veterinary visits
2. **Preventive Care**: Stay current with vaccinations and preventatives
3. **Monitor Health**: Watch for changes in behavior or appetite
4. **Maintain Records**: Keep detailed health documentation
5. **Stay Connected**: Use the app for ongoing support and guidance
